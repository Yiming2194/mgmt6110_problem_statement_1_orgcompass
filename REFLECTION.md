## Who are your users, and what changes for them?
---
**Users:** The product is meant for internal users, particularly CEOs of entities that employed at least 1,000 employees in Singapore.
**Current situation:** **(1)** HR prepares deck of slides containing descriptive information about talent and succession plan **(2)** Executives or business leaders spent 2-3 hours monthly or bi-weekly for roundtable discussions on talent and organization matters, and they are required to make decisions that matter to the business priorities. **(4)** Decisions made are sometimes worked in silos within the department/function of an organization **(3)** Alignment of the talent & organizational development strategy to business priorities are often diluted by HR driven objectives.
**What OrgCompass do better:** **(1)** Show to the Executives talent or organization challenges that could hinder progress to meeting business priorities **(2)** Helicopter view of the challenges enable decisions to be made according to business priorities **(3) Decisions are more business driven than HR driven.
